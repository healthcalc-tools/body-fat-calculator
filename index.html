How to measure

HeightWithout shoes. NeckJust below the larynx/Adam’s apple, tape level. WaistAt navel level, relaxed, after a normal exhalation. HipsAround the widest part of the buttocks.
For tracking: measure at roughly the same time of day and use the same tape position and technique each time.
This is an estimate rather than a direct measurement such as DEXA. Small differences in tape placement can shift the result.
About this estimate

This calculator uses a circumference-based body-fat equation. It is useful for following trends, but it is not a direct measurement such as DEXA and an individual result can be several percentage points away from true body fat.

The coloured Athletic/Fit/Average/High bands are broad adult fitness categories and are not age-adjusted. If you enter your age, the calculator also shows a separate age-and-sex health context based on provisional reference ranges published by Gallagher and colleagues.

Gallagher et al., American Journal of Clinical Nutrition (2000)
